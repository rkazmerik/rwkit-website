01

Analytics Overhaul

Deep dive · 01

Rebuilding a manual reporting workflow into a platform built for both BI and AI

Situation

A sports & entertainment media company had no reusable data model: every request came in, someone hand-wrote SQL, and the answer went back as a static spreadsheet.

Approach

Built a modern AWS-native data stack — Airflow, dbt, S3/Iceberg, Athena, ClickHouse — modeling data in a one-big-table (OBT) design optimized for both BI and LLM consumption.

Why it worked

An Iceberg-based, Airflow/dbt-orchestrated architecture kept the platform cost-effective at high data volume, without a premature data lake.

Eliminated the manual SQL-to-spreadsheet workflow entirely, at roughly 60% lower infrastructure cost than a comparable warehouse-based approach.

02

Agentic AI Data Stack

Deep dive · 02

An agentic AI data stack built on a durable foundation — not just dashboards

Situation

A growing SaaS platform company wanted more than dashboards — AI agents that could act directly on customer and product data, without standing up a fragile stack to get there.

Approach

Foundation first: dbt-driven transforms on a dedicated Postgres database, fed near-real-time from event streams, with row-level security enforcing tenant isolation. The agentic layer was built directly on top of that governed foundation — developed AI-natively using Claude Code.

Why it worked

A durable foundation meant the agentic layer had something reliable to act on from day one. One domain was proven end-to-end before expanding, so the pattern was validated before it was repeated.

A phased roadmap the client could execute with confidence: MVP in 6–8 weeks, expansion in 10–12 weeks, the agentic layer added only once the foundation was proven.

03

Marketing Intelligence

Deep dive · 03

Wiring the acquisition funnel into an AI-powered marketing intelligence and segmentation engine

Situation

An ad tech company's acquisition data lived inside each paid media platform, disconnected from what users actually did once they arrived.

Approach

Wired Meta, TikTok, and Google Ad Manager into the data lake, joining acquisition with engagement data. LiveRamp handled identity resolution, and the unified model fed an LLM layer for marketing intelligence and segmentation.

Why it worked

One identity-resolved view of every user, from first impression to ongoing engagement — so segments were built on behaviour, not just clicks.

A marketing intelligence engine that turns unified acquisition and engagement data into targeted advertising segments, built and scaled through identity resolution.
