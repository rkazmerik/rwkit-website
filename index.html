Modernization · 01

From hand-built reports to a platform the whole business could use

Situation

A media company answered every data question by hand: a request came in, someone wrote a one-off query, and the answer went back as a static spreadsheet. Nothing was reusable.

Approach

Designed and built a modern cloud data platform around a single governed model, one source of truth serving dashboards, self-serve analysis, and AI alike. The architecture was chosen for the real workload, not the fashionable one.

Eliminated the manual reporting workflow entirely, at roughly 60% lower infrastructure cost than the conventional approach.
AI Adoption · 02

An AI roadmap built on a foundation that could carry it

Situation

A growing SaaS company wanted more than dashboards: AI that could act directly on customer and product data, without standing up a fragile stack to get there.

Approach

Foundation first: a governed, near-real-time data layer with strict customer isolation. Then the AI capability built directly on top of it, with one domain proven end-to-end before expanding.

A phased roadmap the client could execute with confidence: MVP in 6–8 weeks, expansion in 10–12 weeks, AI added only once the foundation was proven.
Growth · 03

Turning disconnected marketing data into a revenue engine

Situation

An advertising business's acquisition data lived inside each media platform it bought from, disconnected from what customers actually did once they arrived.

Approach

Unified acquisition and engagement data into one identity-resolved view of every customer, then built an AI-driven intelligence and segmentation layer on top so segments were based on behaviour, not just clicks.

Audience segments scaled from 20K to 2M users, doubling the brand deals the business could unlock.
